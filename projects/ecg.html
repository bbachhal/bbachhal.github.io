ECG Signal Processing & HRV Analysis

Extracting reliable physiological insight from raw, noisy three-lead ECG data using a MATLAB signal processing pipeline, rather than working from idealized datasets.

RoleSole analyst and developer
SoftwareMATLAB
ConditionsRest, post-exertion, box breathing
FocusFiltering, peak detection, feature extraction, HRV

Overview and Motivation

This project investigates how physiological states influence cardiac behavior by analyzing real-world electrocardiogram data. Using MATLAB, I processed three-lead ECG recordings collected under resting, post-exercise, and controlled-breathing conditions to quantify changes in heart rate variability and cardiac cycle dynamics.

As a student interested in medical technology, I wanted to explore where engineering and device development could have real impact. Heart rate variability is an important indicator of autonomic nervous system activity and cardiovascular health, but subtle changes in HRV are usually obscured by noise and variability in real recordings. The challenge here was extracting reliable physiological insight from raw biosignals through robust processing, instead of leaning on clean preprocessed data.

Data Acquisition

ECG data were collected from human subjects using a three-lead configuration, giving enough resolution to analyze waveform morphology and timing. Recordings were taken across three conditions: resting baseline, immediately following physical exertion, and during box breathing to induce controlled respiratory modulation. These conditions allowed direct comparison of autonomic response and cardiac dynamics.

Signal Processing and Feature Extraction

I developed a MATLAB pipeline to clean and analyze the signals:

  • Signal smoothing and noise reduction to improve waveform clarity
  • Peak detection algorithms to identify R-peaks and segment cardiac cycles
  • Feature extraction of P waves, QRS complexes, and T waves, correlating electrical activity to atrial and ventricular function

The pipeline was designed to balance sensitivity against robustness, so detection stays accurate across varying heart rates and signal quality.

Heart Rate Variability Analysis

HRV was quantified by calculating RR intervals between successive heartbeats, then analyzed across conditions to evaluate the effects of exercise-induced stress and controlled breathing on cardiac rhythm. The analysis revealed measurable differences in beat-to-beat variability, highlighting the physiological impact of both exertion and respiratory control.
