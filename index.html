Mechanical Engineering Intern · Sydney, Australia

Designed the enclosure and thermal hardware for a diamond-based quantum magnetometer, a sensor developed for GPS-denied navigation and detection. Because sensor accuracy degrades with temperature, thermal was a functional requirement rather than a survival one.

  1. Designed an IP65-rated ruggedized enclosure in SolidWorks for 24-hour continuous field deployment above 40 °C.

  4. Released a complete production documentation package covering fastener standardization, heat-set insert specifications, and assembly drawings, which an external supplier built to print with zero engineering follow-up.
